SCROOM MACRO - PATCH NOTES
==========================

v2.1
----
The accuracy update. Every minigame was re-measured against recordings using
the game's own Perfect / Good / Miss marks, and a practice range (simulated
minigames, in the source code) now tests the macro at different window sizes,
frame rates, PC speeds, input delays and sceneries.
- Keys: aims at the lettered keys now. It used to press while the tile was
  still in the row above them, which the game only scores "Good" (and misses
  when the tiles are fast, like on Epic and rarer scrooms).
- Count-in: clicks when the ring lies on the circle (yellow dot = Perfect).
  It used to click a moment early, which only gets the green dot.
- Strings: aims at the middle of the Perfect window, and the auto-tune now
  also notices presses that are too early (before, it could only see late ones
  and kept pushing the timing earlier).
- Fixed: a note right behind another one in the same lane was pressed late or
  not at all (strings and keys), and its press was dropped completely with a
  low Latency setting.
- Percussion: clicks were late because a note half hidden under the scroom was
  read wrong. Notes are now placed by their visible edge.
- Wind: follows notes that slide from one line to another, keeping the click
  held all the way.
- Hold notes (strings and keys): let go when the bar ends instead of a bit
  before it.
- Cast ring: learns your PC's delay from every cast now ("Couldn't see where
  the click landed" is gone), and no longer clicks a pass it can't hit on time.
- Presses and clicks are sent at the exact moment, also when the PC stutters.
- Notes are timed from all of their readings and the speed the notes share,
  which keeps presses steady on slow PCs.
- Keys and wind are found on snow and other bright scenery.
- The log tells how the game scored strings and keys, e.g. "12 notes pressed:
  11 Perfect, 1 Good". Discord session reports show the totals.
- Count-in: the circle often wasn't found at all on small game windows
  (1280x720 and smaller - its outline is hardly more than a pixel wide there)
  and was found late on bigger ones, so the first rings, or all four, went by
  unclicked. It's picked up as soon as it appears now, and its size is
  measured to a fraction of a pixel: on a small window the Perfect click is
  only a couple of pixels wide, and clicks came up to 50 ms late there.
  One misread ring can't send a click at an odd moment any more.
- Fast scrooms: when tiles fell more than about twice as fast as a common
  scroom's, keys stopped pressing altogether (it took them for the board's
  closing animation). All four minigames were tested with notes up to three
  times faster (keys, strings and percussion up to four times).
- No key stays down after a minigame: when the catch is complete in the
  middle of a hold note, the key goes up as soon as the board starts closing
  (a held A/S/D walks the character). Same when you click into another window
  or close the app while it plays. And no key is pressed for a note that's
  left over while the strings highway or the keys board closes.
- The macro's own window: pinned on top of the middle of the game, it hid the
  cast ring, the count-in and the minigames from the macro too ("Ring didn't
  appear", "No minigame showed up"). It now steps aside when you press Start,
  and the log says so when it's in the way. Unpinned, it no longer blanks out
  a piece of the game.
- "Ring didn't appear" three casts in a row: the macro presses the instrument
  key (the instrument wasn't in hand - it was already out when the macro
  started, or the game put it away).
- Fixed: memory use creeping up during a long session.
- Fixed: keys hold notes let go late on big windows; hold notes on fast
  scrooms let go late.
- Fixed: a press coming late after a stutter right before it, a fast note
  skipped after a stutter, and a press coming late when a hold note in
  another lane ended at the same moment.
- Faster: keys are read about 40% faster, and the search for the count-in and
  the minigame after a cast takes a third of the time.
- Count-in: gives up after a few seconds without rings instead of sitting
  there for 12, and aims at the middle of where clicks got the yellow dot on
  recordings (a little further out than before).
- App: the "Running" and "Per hour" tiles stop when the macro stops; what it
  learned (latency, note timing) is saved when it stops, not only when the
  app is closed; one damaged value in settings.json no longer resets all
  settings.

v2.0.2
------
- Fixed: the third count-in ring was clicked too early (it showed red), and
  the fourth ring was sometimes not clicked at all.
- Fixed: strings weren't played in places full of one lane's color (yellow
  sand, red rock, green grass) - the macro said "No minigame showed up".
- Keys: hold notes (the tile with a grey bar above it) are held until the bar
  ends.

v2.0.1
------
- Fixed: the character walked a bit after a keys minigame (the macro pressed
  keys for leftover tiles while the board was closing).
- Fixed: tuning and count-in clicks are steadier - the learned latency no
  longer jumps around from one cast to the next.
- Fixed: Roblox started from Voidstrap/Bloxstrap with a renamed window is found.
- Keys: special ability notes (the sword and the blue icon note) are played.
- Fixed: the tuning (cast) ring wasn't found when the camera was turned and
  the ring floated near the side of the screen.
- Fixed: starting the macro shrank a maximized/fullscreen Roblox window.
- Added: minigame keybinds setting (General tab) for players who changed
  their strings/keys buttons in the game.
- Fixed: on small screens the app ran under the taskbar - the settings now
  scroll and the window stays on screen.

v2.0
----
- Brand new look: dark, cleaner app with a status card, stats and settings tabs.
- Live minigame view: watch the notes, the circle, the count-in and the reel
  as the macro plays them.
- Count-in support: the macro now clicks the head-start rings before every
  minigame.
- Wind instruments: the macro steers the scroom onto each note's line with
  the mouse and holds left click through the note.
- Discord alerts: paste a webhook in the Discord tab to get every catch posted
  with a screenshot, plus a session report every few minutes.
- Update alerts: the app tells you when a new version is out.
- Strings hold notes (the long bars) are held until the bar ends.
- Strings notes are hit more on time.
- Works with the macro window on top of the game.
- Strings and count-in are found more reliably, also in small game windows.
- Strings/Keys notes are timed better for more Perfects, plus a Note timing setting.
- Note timing auto-tune: the macro checks when the strings circles light up
  and corrects its timing toward Perfect by itself.
- Notes are pressed at the exact moment instead of on the next screen read.
- Discord pings: add your Discord user ID to get pinged when the macro stops
  by itself (Roblox closed, errors, catch goal reached) or gets stuck.
- Pin button keeps the app on top of the game, plus a mini view.
- Cleaner settings with on/off switches.
- Cleaner download: just ScroomMacro.exe, its _internal folder and a README.
- Stats now show catches, casts, catches per hour and how long it's been running.
- Works on every keyboard layout (it presses the physical A/S/D/F keys).

v1.9.3
------
- Updated for the Crescendo Valley update.
- Added: Strings instruments (press A/S/D when the notes reach the circles).
- Added: Keys instruments (press A/S/D/F when the tiles reach the bottom row).
- Added: Percussion instruments (left click white notes, right click blue
  notes, both for big notes).
- Wind instruments are not supported yet.
- The first time you use an instrument, click "Continue" on the game's
  tutorial yourself - the macro waits for it.

v1.9.2
------
- Fixed "Roblox isn't the active window" showing even when you're playing.
  This happened if another window was also called "Roblox" (Bloxstrap, a
  browser tab, the Microsoft Store version...). The macro now uses the
  Roblox window you're actually in.
- New "Make bug report" button (bottom left). If something goes wrong,
  click it and send the file it makes. When the macro has a problem (circle
  not clicked, frequency lost, scroom got away...) it now saves a picture of
  the screen, so the bug report shows exactly what went wrong.

v1.9.1
------
- Fixed: the app crashing on some PCs with an error about "numpy" when you
  open it. It now starts up in a safer way.
- If the app still can't start (for example your antivirus removed one of its
  files), you now get a clear message telling you how to fix it, instead of a
  confusing error. It also saves startup_error.txt that you can send for help.
- Added simple install steps to the top of the README.

v1.9
----
- Works in very bright places (like snowy areas). Before, the macro could stop
  doing anything mid-catch and let the scroom go.
- Works with every reel box size (it can change with your gear/upgrades).
- Better at clicking the circle in bright places.

v1.8
----
- Works with rarer scrooms that turn the reel purple (or other colors).
  Before, the dot could slide to the left and lose the catch.
- Fixed the dot swinging right then left right before a catch finished.
- Works when bright grass is behind the reel.

v1.7
----
- Removed a part of the code that some antivirus programs mistook for a
  keylogger. Fewer false virus warnings.

v1.6
----
- The app is now a folder (App) instead of one file - fewer antivirus warnings.
- The start/stop key (F6) works in a safer way.

v1.5
----
- Failsafe: if the circle part gets stuck for more than 5 seconds, the macro
  puts your instrument away, takes it out again and casts again.

v1.4
----
- Circle clicks are much more accurate, and the macro now learns your PC's
  timing by itself after each cast.

v1.3 and earlier
----------------
- First versions: automatic casting, circle, frequency and reel; works at any
